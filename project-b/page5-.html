The Kǎn trigram, represented by one broken line at the top, one solid line in the middle, and one broken line at the bottom (☵), symbolizes Water or Danger. It represents the unknown, the hidden, and challenges that lie beneath the surface. Water is both life-giving and potentially destructive, and Kǎn is associated with obstacles, risks, and the need for careful navigation. It reflects the need to approach uncertain situations with caution, wisdom, and adaptability, especially when dealing with emotional or difficult matters.



波峰为阳，波谷为阴。潮起潮落，阴阳相佐。

The peak of the wave is Yang, and the trough is Yin. Ebb and flow, Yin and Yang.
