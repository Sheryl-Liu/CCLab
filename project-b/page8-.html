☱

The Duì trigram, represented by two solid lines on top and one broken line at the bottom (☱), symbolizes Lake or Joy. It represents openness, communication, and joy in both emotional and social contexts. Duì embodies the spirit of harmony and pleasure, encouraging connection and the expression of happiness. This trigram is associated with sharing, contentment, and the joys of life. It suggests the importance of positive relationships, mutual support, and a cheerful disposition in life.



呼吸之间，藏阴阳之道。吸为阴，呼为阳；吸纳天地之气，静而收敛，是为阴；吐出浊气，动而散发，是为阳。阴阳交替，循环不息，乃生命之本。

Between the breath, the way of Yin and Yang. Inhalation is Yin, exhalation is Yang; Absorb the qi of heaven and earth, quiet and convergence, is Yin; Exhaling turbid air, moving and emitting, is Yang. The alternation of Yin and Yang, the endless cycle, is the foundation of life.
