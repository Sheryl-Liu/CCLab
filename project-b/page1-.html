The Qián trigram, symbolized by three solid lines (☰), represents Heaven or the Creative principle in the I Ching. It embodies the active, expansive, and masculine energy of the universe. As the ultimate expression of Yang energy, it signifies strength, power, and leadership. Qián is associated with creativity, initiative, and the driving force behind creation and change. This trigram encourages the pursuit of goals with determination, highlighting the importance of willpower, clarity, and confidence in one's endeavors.



当太阳走下山时，正是月亮的升起。而月亮的陨落，正是旭日的东升。太阳为阳，月亮为阴。

When the sun goes down, it is the moon that rises. The fall of the moon is the rise of the sun. The sun is Yang, and the moon is Yin.
