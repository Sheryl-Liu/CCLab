☳

The Zhèn trigram, symbolized by two solid lines on top and a broken line at the bottom (☳), represents Thunder or Arousing. It signifies movement, action, and the power to shock or awaken. This trigram embodies the energy of sudden, forceful change, often creating a new direction or awakening awareness. It is linked with dynamic energy, innovation, and transformation. The presence of Zhèn suggests a call to action, urging one to be proactive and embrace change with courage, even when it comes unexpectedly.



闪电的阳，雷声为阴。他们共同完成了天地间的互动，展示了自然界的能量循环与释放。

Lightning is Yang, thunder is Yin. Together, they completed the interaction between heaven and earth, demonstrating the energy cycle and release of nature.
