☴

The Xùn trigram, represented by one solid line at the top and two broken lines at the bottom (☴), symbolizes Wind or Gentle. It represents the principle of gentle influence and the power of persistence. Wind is a soft force that moves gradually but can eventually shape the landscape over time. This trigram reflects qualities such as flexibility, adaptability, and diplomacy. It suggests that small, continuous actions can lead to significant changes, and emphasizes communication and subtle influence.



四季更替，季节轮回。春夏为阳，秋冬为阴

The seasons come and go. Spring and summer are Yang, autumn and winter are Yin.
