The Lí trigram, represented by one solid line at the top and bottom with a broken line in the middle (☲), symbolizes Fire or Clarity. It represents brightness, clarity, and illumination. Fire burns away impurities and provides light, symbolizing transformation and insight. Lí reflects the pursuit of understanding, knowledge, and passion. It suggests the need to focus on clarity, insight, and transformation, with the light of awareness guiding one's path forward. However, it also warns against losing control of one's emotions or energy, which could lead to destruction



水为阴，火为阳。水火本不相容，却又互相依存。天地之间，水火交融，平衡方成道。

Water is Yin, fire is Yang. Fire and water are incompatible, but they depend on each other. Between heaven and earth, fire and water blend, balance becomes the way.
