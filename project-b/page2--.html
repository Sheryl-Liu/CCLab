The Kūn trigram, represented by three broken lines (☷), stands for the Earth or the Receptive principle. It is the complement of Qián, symbolizing the nurturing, passive, and feminine aspect of the universe. The Earth provides support, stability, and sustenance, allowing things to grow and flourish. Kūn is associated with qualities such as patience, acceptance, and adaptability. This trigram reflects the idea of yielding, allowing one to gain strength by responding to external influences, and emphasizes harmony with the natural world.



女子为阴，男子为阳。一阴一阴之谓非道，一阳一阳之谓非道。

The female is Yin and the male is Yang. One Yin and one Yin are called non-Dao, and one Yang and one Yang are called non-Dao.
