☶

The Gèn trigram, represented by a solid line at the top and two broken lines below (☶), symbolizes Mountain or Stillness. It represents stability, restraint, and the ability to remain firm in the face of challenges. Mountains are immovable and steady, providing a foundation for growth. This trigram signifies the importance of introspection, careful decision-making, and the strength that comes from stillness. It reflects a time for pause, reflection, and grounding before moving forward. Gèn encourages one to maintain composure and to avoid rash decisions.
